I made the blog bilingual so I can keep writing naturally in Romanian without losing readers who prefer English.

Romanian remains the primary language and lives at the root URL. English sits under `/en/`, so it is clearly a separate version rather than mixed language content on the same page.

I do not plan to translate everything automatically. I will add English versions where it makes sense, especially for posts that may also be useful to people outside Romania or Moldova.

What matters most to me is keeping the blog voice natural. If a post works better in one language, I will leave it that way. If it deserves two versions, I will publish both cleanly.
